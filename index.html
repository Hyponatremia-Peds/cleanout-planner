Your child's plan

Part 1: 3-day cleanout (Days 1, 2 and 3)

Part 2: Daily maintenance (starts Day 4)

The maintenance dose is the same single dose used in the cleanout, given once a day (not twice). It is usually needed for at least 6 to 12 months, often longer. You can raise or lower the dose as needed to get soft, mashed-potato-like stools.

Important tips

Mixing and giving the medicines
  • 1 capful of Miralax (polyethylene glycol, also sold as Glycolax or PEG) = 17 grams. Use the cap that comes on the medicine bottle.
  • Mix Miralax in a clear liquid such as juice, water or tea. Do not use milk. It is important to mix it in the full amount of liquid listed.
  • Have your child drink lots of liquids during the cleanout to prevent dehydration.
  • Check the strength on your senna or bisacodyl package. It should match the strength shown in your plan. If it does not, ask your provider or pharmacist before giving it.
What is the goal?
  • Soft stools the consistency of mashed potatoes every day, with no stomach pain and no soiling.
  • During maintenance, you can increase or decrease the daily Miralax dose as needed to reach mashed-potato-consistency stools.
  • If needed, the 3-day cleanout may be repeated in two weeks, or as directed by your provider.
  • If your child is in a lot of distress, ask your provider whether an enema should be added.
When to call your child's healthcare provider
  • Severe stomach pain, or pain that keeps getting worse
  • Vomiting, or a swollen, hard belly
  • Blood in the stool, or black stools
  • No bowel movement by the end of the 3-day cleanout
  • Signs of dehydration: very little urine, dry mouth, no tears, or unusual sleepiness
  • You are not sure about the dose or how to give a medicine

If your child seems very sick, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room.

This planner gives general dosing by weight for a standard 3-day constipation cleanout and maintenance plan. It does not replace advice from your child's healthcare provider. Always follow your provider's instructions if they are different.
